合作故事

DTLR 的 Cornerstone 在经典灰色网眼与深色麂皮间加入紫色、橙色亮点。品牌宣布于 2025 年 9 月 26 日限量发售 500 双。

展开英文原始摘要

DTLR's Cornerstone combines classic grey mesh and dark suede with bright purple and orange accents. DTLR announced a 500-pair release for September 26, 2025.

设计灵感

灰色网眼、海军蓝和黑色麂皮延续 990 的熟悉基调，荧光色鞋舌和 N 字细节打破灰色秩序。

展开英文原始摘要

DTLR frames the grey mesh and navy-black suede as a meeting of its own history with the 990 lineage; highlighter-toned tongue and N details, purple and orange accents interrupt the familiar New Balance grey.

合作背景

DTLR 将这双鞋定位为自家独占的 500 双限定，并通过门店和线上渠道发售。

展开英文原始摘要

The retailer positioned Cornerstone as a DTLR-exclusive 500-pair release, available both in its stores and online rather than a general New Balance launch.
