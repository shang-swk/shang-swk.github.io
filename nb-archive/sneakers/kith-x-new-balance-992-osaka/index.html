合作故事

为 Kith Osaka 开幕而推出的 992，以低饱和蓝绿色网眼搭配麂皮和皮革。实体发售仅在大阪，线上与 App 渠道也提供购买。

展开英文原始摘要

Made for the opening of Kith Osaka, this 992 puts a muted blue-green mesh base beneath premium suede and leather. Kith limited the in-store release to Osaka, while also offering pairs through its online channels and app.

阅读故事来源 ↗

设计灵感

大阪开幕企划以蓝、白、灰构成两双鞋的共同配色；992 与 991v2 都有联名鞋垫和 Kith 后跟细节，但分别保留 Made in USA 与 Made in UK 结构。

展开英文原始摘要

Kith's Osaka opening shaped a two-shoe palette of blue, white and grey; the 992 and 991v2 share co-branded sockliners and Kith heel detailing, but retain their own Made in USA and Made in UK constructions.

合作背景

这双鞋纪念 Kith 在日本的第二间旗舰店。与同期 991v2 相比，992 的线下渠道更具地点属性：Kith 只列出大阪店作为实体发售点。

展开英文原始摘要

The pair marked Kith's second Japanese flagship. The 992 was the more location-specific of the two releases: Kith listed Osaka as its only physical retail location for this model.
